# History of Korea — 3D video

For Before The Fall. 16:9, final cut 6 minutes 30 seconds, no burned-in captions.
Made in 10-second parts, scene by scene, with voiceover for the whole video and no repeated scenes.

## Arc
1. The peninsula before division — geography, kingdoms, identity.
2. Division after World War II — two Koreas, two systems.
3. The Korean War — invasion in 1950, Incheon landing, stalemate.
4. Aftermath — the DMZ, rebuilding, two very different paths.
5. What the division still means today.

## Production notes
- 3D cinematic style: golden-hour city renders and parchment maps.
- Voiceover in every part as well as the final combined video.
- Keep each 10-second part a new visual — no repeated scenes.
