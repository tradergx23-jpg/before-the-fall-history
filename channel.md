# Before The Fall — format checklist

- Cold-open hook: date + place, in the first seconds.
- Thesis: what broke this empire, in one sentence.
- Chapters of ~30 seconds: rise → peak → breaking point → collapse.
- Visuals: golden-hour 3D city renders, parchment maps.
- Voiceover: deep male documentary voice, in every part and the final.
- Long-form: 16:9, no burned-in captions. Shorts: 9:16, ~40 seconds.
- End with title, description and chapters for YouTube.
