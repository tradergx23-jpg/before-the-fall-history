# Before The Fall — History Scripts & Notes

Scripts and research notes for my YouTube channel **Before The Fall** (@Before_the_Fall_of_Empires) — cinematic 3D history of empires in the decades before their fall.

**Style:** golden-hour 3D city renders, parchment maps, cold-open hook with date/place, thesis, ~30-second chapters, rise → peak → breaking point → collapse, deep male documentary voiceover, burned-in captions.

**Topics so far:** Rome, Russia. **Next:** Byzantium, Ottomans, British Empire.
**Format:** long-form 16:9 (5–7 min) and 9:16 Shorts (~40 sec).

## Contents
- `korea-history.md` — History of Korea video: from division through the Korean War and the DMZ. Final cut 6m 30s, 16:9, no burned-in captions.
- `channel.md` — channel format, structure and checklist for each video.
